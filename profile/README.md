# Esiana TTRPG

A self-hosted campaign management platform focused on worldbuilding, lore organization, and long-term narrative continuity.

## Repositories

### 📦 esiana-core

The primary Esiana application.

Esiana provides tools for campaign management, worldbuilding, codex organization, maps, timelines, session tracking, and narrative continuity. It is designed for Game Masters who want a persistent knowledge and storytelling platform rather than a virtual tabletop.

Key areas include:

- Campaign and party management
- Worldbuilding and wiki
- Characters, organizations, locations, and maps
- Multiple timelines support and session tracking
- Knowledge discovery and player-facing content
- Self-hosted deployment with SQLite or PostgreSQL

→ https://github.com/Esiana-ttrpg/esiana-core
